SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement is made on 12 January 2026 between Al Waha Medical Supplies W.L.L., a company registered in Doha, Qatar ("the Supplier"), and Corniche Clinics Group LLC, a company registered in Abu Dhabi ("the Buyer").

Article 1 – Definitions
In this Agreement, "Products" means the medical consumables listed in Schedule A.

Article 2 – Term and Renewal
2.1 This Agreement starts on 01/02/2026 and continues for a period of twelve (12) months.
2.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry.

Article 3 – Payment
3.1 The Buyer shall pay the Supplier a fixed fee of QAR 150,000 per quarter.
3.2 Each invoice is payable within 30 days of receipt. Late payments carry a charge of USD 250 per week of delay.

Article 4 – Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed by the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party for 3 years after termination of this Agreement.

Article 5 – Termination
5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by giving 30 days written notice.
5.2 The Buyer may terminate this Agreement at any time at its sole discretion without prior notice.

Article 6 – Liability
The Supplier shall be liable for all losses and damages arising from defective Products, and its liability shall be unlimited liability for that purpose.

Article 7 – Force Majeure
Neither party is responsible for delay caused by force majeure, including events beyond its reasonable control such as natural disasters or government action.

Article 8 – Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Qatar.

Article 9 – Dispute Resolution
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be settled amicably and, failing that, by arbitration at the Qatar International Center for Conciliation and Arbitration.
